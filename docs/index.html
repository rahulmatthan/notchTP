Eye contact, effortlessly

Text scrolls directly below the camera so you appear to look right at your audience.

⌨️

Keyboard control

Space to play/pause. Arrow keys to nudge speed. R to reset. Esc to close.

📋

Paste any script

Copy your text anywhere, tap the settings icon, hit Paste. Done.

🖥

Lives in the menu bar

No Dock icon. One click to show or hide. Stays out of your way until you need it.

🎞

Smooth animation

The panel emerges from behind the notch when you open it and retreats when you close.

🎛

Adjustable speed & size

Dial in your scroll speed and font size from the settings flyout.

How to install

1
Download the DMG Click the download button above and open the .dmg file.
2
Copy to Applications Drag MacbookTeleprompter.app into your Applications folder.
3
Bypass Gatekeeper (first launch only) Because the app isn't notarized, right-click → Open instead of double-clicking, then click Open in the dialog. You only need to do this once.
4
Look for the icon in your menu bar Click it to show the teleprompter. Paste your script from the ⚙ settings panel and press Space.
